A single-arm session ignores the other hand entirely — nothing is ever written to the arm it has none for. In the behind stance the arm under your right hand is the one named left; the selectors above are labelled by hand, not by arm id, so pick the arm you can see under that hand.

Enter VR

Grip = clutch (hold to drive; release, reposition, grip again). Trigger = gripper. A / X = precision (hold for fine work). B / Y = E-STOP. Thumbstick click = settings panel; push the stick to move through it and change values.
